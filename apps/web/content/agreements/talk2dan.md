Parties: This agreement is between SoundBridge Live Ltd (UK company number 16854928) ("SoundBridge") and Talk 2 Dan, a creative community based in London ("Talk 2 Dan"), represented by the signer named at the foot of this agreement. The signer confirms they are authorised to enter into this agreement for Talk 2 Dan.

2. Referral and tracking: SoundBridge provides Talk 2 Dan with a unique referral link. Sign-ups made through that link are attributed to Talk 2 Dan.

3. Commission: SoundBridge pays Talk 2 Dan a 10% recurring commission on subscription payments from subscribers referred through the link, for as long as each referred subscriber remains subscribed, from the moment the subscriber joins.

4. Increase: The commission increases to 12% once a milestone is reached. The milestone will be agreed in writing by both parties.

5. Commission base and payment: Commission is 10% of the subscription price paid by the subscriber. SoundBridge pays commission after its monthly accounting is complete.

6. No acquisition fee: There is no sign-up fee, acquisition fee or per-subscriber payment other than the recurring commission described above.

7. Cancellations and refunds: No commission is payable on subscriptions that have been cancelled, or on payments that have been refunded or reversed, after that point.

8. Term and ending: Either party may end this arrangement by written notice. Commission earned before ending remains payable.

9. Conduct: Talk 2 Dan will represent SoundBridge accurately and will not make guarantees about earnings or features.
